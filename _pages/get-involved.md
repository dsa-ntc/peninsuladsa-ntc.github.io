### Join DSA

You can be officially a part of the Peninsula DSA chapter by **living or working in San Mateo County** under the region we cover, and **become a member the DSA** and [paying dues to the national organization](https://act.dsausa.org/donate/membership/). 20% of your monthly dues gets allocated back to our chapter. If you aren't sure if you live in an area we cover, check the [About Us](/about) page for a map of our chapter's area, or use the [Chapter Lookup Tool](https://chapters.dsausa.org/) to see if your local DSA chapter is us.

Not ready to join yet? You are still welcome to [meet with us](#meet-your-comrades) even if you aren't a member!

You would have two options when signing up:
- The Monthly Membership option has you choose a set amount for monthly dues
- The Solidarity Dues option has you choose a percentage of your annual income

**No one is turned away for lack of funds.** If you’d like to join but cannot afford to contribute financially, please complete this [dues waiver form](https://act.dsausa.org/survey/dueswaiver).

If you have any other questions about your DSA membership or signing up, please start [here](https://www.dsausa.org/resources/faq/).

#### One Week After Joining

At maximum one week after you sign up with national, our chapter is sent your information, and our Membership co-chairs will connect you to our local organizing work and invite you to the PDSA Discord server, our virtual HQ for coordinating between meetings and events.

Signed up and still waiting for us to reach out? Shoot us an email at [info@peninsuladsa.org](mailto:info@peninsuladsa.org)

### Meet Your Comrades!

You are welcome to our in-person events, even if:
* You haven't signed up
* You're still waiting for your membership to process
* You've reached out but haven't quite heard from us yet

_(Note that all remote events that happen on Discord are available to members only, so you'll have to sign up for DSA membership to access them)_

Join us for some socialist reading, a Friday social, a nice hike, a movie night or a general meeting! We can talk about what's going on in the world, and what us Democratic Socialists in San Mateo County can do about it.

#### Things you can do

- **Check out [this page](/events)** to see upcoming events that you can join.
- **Subscribe to our [mailing list and chapter newsletter](/subscribe)** to stay up-to-date on our work and get reminders about upcoming events
- **Take a look at the [areas of our work](/groups/)** to see what you might be interested in contributing to and helping to lead.
- **Join our in person [reading group](/reading-group/)** to study political theory / history and get dinner with us after.
