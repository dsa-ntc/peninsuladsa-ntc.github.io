## Best for Prospective & New Members: {#events}

_If you want to know more about democratic socialism, are considering joining DSA, or you recently signed up for membership, check out these events:_

### :rose: First Friday Socials <!-- ("/meet/#first-fridays) -->

>I did not believe that a Cause which stood for a beautiful ideal, for anarchism, for release and freedom from convention and prejudice, should demand the denial of life and joy. <br> -- Emma Goldman, _Living My Life_ (1931)

Come join your comrades after work to talk about socialism! Each month, we alternate between bayside and coastside locations, and usually pick a venue (with outdoor seating) where under 21s are welcome.

### :rose: [Reading Group](../reading-group/)

The people who came before us have a lot to teach us! Our political [reading group](../reading-group/) meets twice a month in Redwood City to discuss socialist books and articles democratically chosen by participants. We read everything from Karl Marx to Marta Harnecker to Malcolm Harris.
>_Read more [here](../reading-group/) or on the **PDSA Discord: #reading-group**_.

## Chapter Meetings

**New and non-members welcome!** Our chapter's monthly general meetings, usually held on the 3rd Sunday from 3-5pm, are key to coordinating our organizing efforts. We host presentations on socialist topics, hold democratic discussions (and vote!) on chapter priorities, and always share updates from our Steering Committee and working groups. If you're a chapter member, attending general meetings is the best way to stay engaged and up-to-date! 

> _Chapter Meetings are held at the Unitarian Universalist Church of San Mateo, every third Sunday of the month, from 3:00-5:00pm._

## Working Groups and Committees

Our [**working groups**](/groups/#working-groups) organize socialist campaigns along various terrains of struggle in the class war. Working group meetings are the main time and place where we organize for our political action. If you want to contribute to our political activity, develop yourself as an organizer, and/or help steer new political activity, these meetings are where you want to be.

Our [**committees**](/groups/#committees) - namely membership, communications, and (in development) political education - perform the tasks necessary for the maintenance, growth and development of the chapter membership and its organizing capacity. If you want to help build the strength of PDSA as an vehicle for class struggle in the Bay Area, come and contribute your skills at commmitee committee.

>_Working Group and Committee meetings are usually held virtually on the **PDSA Discord: #general**._
