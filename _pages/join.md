<h3> Come Hang Out!</h3>
Join us for some socialist reading, a Friday social, a nice hike, or a movie night. Then, we can talk about the what's going on in the world and what us Democratic Socialists in San Mateo County can do about it.

To learn where and when you can do so:

>[**Check out this page**](../events) to see our event calendar and learn about what kind of events we are hosting.

>[**Subscribe to our monthly email newsletter**](http://eepurl.com/cNwNHH) to keep up to date on our current activities and upcoming events

Everyone is welcome at our in-person events, **even if you're not a member or you're still waiting for your membership to process** 

_Unfortunately, we can't add people to the PDSA Discord until they are members, so you'll have to sign up for DSA membership through the national org. to attend remote events_

<h3>Sign-Up for DSA Membership</h3>

>[Become a member of DSA by paying dues to the national organization. 20% of your monthly dues gets allocated back to our chapter.](https://act.dsausa.org/donate/membership/)

**Note about signing up:**

* **Monthly Membership** option has you choose a set amount for monthly dues

* **Solidarity Dues** option has you choose a percentage of your annual income

>[**No one is turned away for lack of funds.** If you’d like to join but cannot afford to contribute financially, please complete this dues waiver form](https://act.dsausa.org/survey/dueswaiver).


<h3>One Week Later...</h3>
At maximum one week after you sign up with national, our chapter is sent your information, and our Membership co-chairs will connect you to our local organizing work and invite you to the PDSA Discord server, our virtual HQ for coordinating between meetings and events.

>[If you have any other questions about your DSA membership or signing up, please start here](dsausa.org/resources/faq)
